# Muskrat Falls web edition

The web edition summarizes the 17-page April 2025 paper and keeps its original PDF and permalink. It does not update the project to present-day figures.

- **Network:** Appendix A, PDF p. 16, and the description on pp. 2–3 inform the schematic and capacity and link lengths. The schematic is an original diagram of connectivity, not a tracing or a geographic map.
- **Estimates:** Paper p. 6 and inquiry vol. 1, p. 2. Sanction total is 6.2 + 1.2 = 7.4. Inquiry project-only total is 10.1 + 2.6 = 12.7. Change is (12.7 − 7.4) / 7.4 ≈ 71.6%. All amounts are Canadian dollars.
- **Allocation table:** Paper Appendix B, PDF p. 17. The entries were cross-checked against inquiry vol. 2, Figure 2.21, p. 160. The inquiry table breaks the project-side 2.6 into 1.8 in interest/allowances and 0.8 in other costs. Maritime Link costs add 1.75 to the 12.70 project total. A blank value in the CSV corresponds to a dash in the source table.
- **Governance and environment:** Paper pp. 3–13. The web text attributes the financing and consultation analysis to the paper and distinguishes a mitigation proposal from an observed outcome.
