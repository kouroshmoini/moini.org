Chinatown Gateway Public Realm Study

A study of how people experience Chinatown’s streets and public spaces, with recommendations for pedestrian access, safety, and streetscape improvements.

Project
Year-long capstone
Institution
Concordia University
Timeline
September 2023 – May 2024

Fieldwork

I conducted land-use observations, pedestrian accessibility audits, and stakeholder interviews to identify barriers in the public realm and understand community priorities.

Mapping

I organized the spatial findings in ArcGIS Online so the team could connect observations and interview findings to locations in the study area.

Recommendations

We developed recommendations for accessibility, safety, and streetscape improvements, and shared the findings through a research poster and presentation.
